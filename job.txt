Your area of work:
As a Business Analyst at FundsDLT, you will join a pioneering team dedicated
to modernizing the global investment fund industry through the power of
blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT). In this role, you will
operate at the intersection of cutting-edge technology and next-generation
asset management, helping financial institutions, asset managers, and wealth
distributors transition from complex legacy systems to our decentralized,
cloud-native platform. You will collaborate closely with cross-functional
teams—including product owners, software engineers, and key external
clients—to analyze market demands and translate complex financial workflows
into seamless digital solutions. Your work will directly impact how the fund
distribution value chain is reengineered, making transaction processing,
custody, and digital compliance faster, cheaper, and more transparent for the
entire industry.
Your responsibilities:
• Gather, analyze, and define business requirements to optimize and scale
our B2B fund distribution platform.
• Map end-to-end user journeys for diverse stakeholders, including asset
managers, wealth distributors, and transfer agents.
• Translate business requirements into detailed functional specifications,
user stories, and acceptance criteria for development squads.
• Support the design and implementation of new platform features, API
integrations, and digital compliance workflows (such as AML and MiFID).
• Act as a vital bridge between business stakeholders and technology teams
to ensure alignment on product delivery.
• Conduct impact analyses and facilitate stakeholder workshops to refine
product features and platform enhancements.
Your profile:
• University degree in Business Administration, Finance, Computer Science,
or a related field.
• Minimum of 3 years of experience as a Business Analyst, preferably within
fintech, asset management, or financial services.
• Strong understanding of fund distribution mechanisms, transfer agency
operations, or post-trade processes.
• Keen interest in or prior experience with blockchain technology, DLT,
smart contracts, or API-driven architectures.
• Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a proven ability to
translate complex requirements into structured documentation.
• Fluent English communication skills (written and spoken), with the
ability to bridge the gap between technical and non-technical stakeholders.
• A collaborative team player who thrives in a fast-paced, innovative, and
agile environment.
